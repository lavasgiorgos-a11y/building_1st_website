About Annita

Annita Tiki was born into a musical family as the daughter of renowned Greek singer and songwriter John Tikis, one of Greece's most celebrated international performers with 8 Gold Records and 15 Albums to his credit. Growing up surrounded by melodies and music, she naturally gravitated toward the arts, pursuing higher studies in piano and music theory. For years, Annita has worked as a piano teacher in the southern suburbs of Athens, particularly in Voula, sharing her love and passion for music with children and adults alike. Her approach to teaching reflects her belief that music is not just an art form but a powerful educational tool that develops intelligence, creativity, and social skills. However, Annita's journey took a transformative turn when she faced a difficult period with health challenges. This experience changed her perspective on life and awakened her to the true power she held within herself—a power we all possess. It was through this personal transformation that writing entered her life, and she discovered her calling as an author and guide for others seeking healing and self-discovery.

Family Heritage

Annita comes from a distinguished artistic family. Her father, John Tikis (Tzon Tikis), is one of Greece's most celebrated international performers, with 8 Gold Records and 15 albums spanning a career of over five decades. He has performed throughout the world—in Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, the USA, Greece, and Asia—and hosted the television show 'Let's Go Greek, Endaxi' on Channel 10 for three years in the 1980s. Her sister, Angela Tiki, is a respected astrologer who has supported Annita's book presentations and journey. Growing up in a home 'full of melodies,' Annita absorbed the artistic spirit and creative passion that now infuses all aspects of her work.

Her family's legacy of artistry and resilience continues to inspire her commitment to music, writing, and guiding others toward transformative growth.

My Book: 'Αγαπημένο μου DNA' (My Beloved DNA)

Published by Lebee Publications, 2025

Annita's first novel, based on her own powerful true story. The book follows Eva, a young woman full of dreams who suddenly faces a life-changing trial. She moves forward, fights, and creates a beautiful reality, but fears and doubts continue to weigh on her. Twenty years later, life brings her back to the same point—but this time, she is different. The novel explores profound questions: Will she succeed? Will she follow the signs showing her the way, or will she lose herself again in the shadows of the past? Can love become the force that breaks down her old beliefs and leads her to a new beginning? How determined she is to discover her true self will determine everything. A moving novel about the power of the soul, awakening through trials, and the choices that define our lives.

"Writing came into my life after a difficult period with health issues, which made me see life from a different perspective and discover the real strength I held within me—as we all do. Through my first book, I want to offer a different view of life and help readers find the strength and peace within themselves."
